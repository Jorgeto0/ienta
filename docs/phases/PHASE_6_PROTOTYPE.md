# Phase 6 — Product Prototype

## Purpose

Integrate all layers into a panel-ready unit suitable for installation
in a real residential electrical panel.

---

## Scope

- Custom PCB replacing Nucleo development board
- Panel-rated enclosure with proper isolation and clearances
- Hardware watchdog timer for autonomous recovery
- Field-upgradeable firmware over UART or USB
- Full system validation under real load conditions
- Safety certification requirements review
